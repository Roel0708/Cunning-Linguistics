Fictional Languages

Lord of the Rings

Elvish from The Lord of the Rings series was created by author J.R.R. Tolkien, a philologist who drew inspiration from Finnish and Welsh.

The two Elvish dialects, Quenya and Sindarin, are fully functional languages with their own grammar and vocabulary.

Fans have even translated poetry and religious texts into Quenya and Sindarin, revealing the depth of these languages.

Dwarvish is primarily Khuzdul (also spelled Khuzdûl), the secret spoken tongue of the Dwarves.

A Song of Ice and Fire

In his various works set in the world of Westeros, author George R. R. Martin has created multiple languages.

High Valyrian, the language of the old Valyrian Freehold, is learned by well-educated men across many of its former territories and beyond

In the four centuries since Valyria's destruction, everyday use led the language spoken in different former provinces to grow into a dozen bastardized dialects from the Free Cities to Slaver's Bay, known as "Low Valyrian".

The Dothraki language is the tongue of the Dothraki, the indigenous inhabitants of the Dothraki sea, a vast inland area of grasslands.

Star Trek

In the Star Trek universe, dozens of alien and human languages exist. Most are bridged on screen by the Universal Translator.

Klingon is the most complete Star Trek language, it has its own grammar, vocabulary, and script, used by the Klingons.

Vulcan is spoken by the Vulcans, an extraterrestrial species known for logic, emotional suppression, and pointed ears, hailing from the planet Vulcan.

Romulan is a language used in the Romulan Star Empire, appearing in shows and expanded heavily in tie-in novels.

Star Wars

Star Wars features dozens of constructed and alien languages, led by Galactic Basic, which uses the Aurebesh script.

Shyriiwook is spoken by Wookiees from Kashyyyk (such as Chewbacca), using growls and roars.

The language spoken by Tatooine's Jawas, 'Jawaese', was created from sped-up Zulu audio.

Huttese is widely spoken across the criminal underworld and Hutt space. Included is an example of a character speaking Huttese that recently went viral.
