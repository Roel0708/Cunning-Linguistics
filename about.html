About Cunning Linguistics

Cunning Linguistics started as an idea to combine my longstanding affinity for languages with my newfound pursuit of a career in IT.

Having previously explored the world of languages, especially English, I wanted to create something that would allow me to reconnect with that interest while developing my skills in web development.

Language comes in many different forms. From the natural languages spoken across the globe to the fictional tongues of imagined worlds and the programming languages that power modern technology, each offers its own unique way of expressing ideas and communicating information.

Cunning Linguistics aims to bring these seemingly different worlds together in one place, offering an accessible introduction to their origins, characteristics and uses. Whether you are interested in the diversity of human languages, the creativity behind fictional languages or the logic of programming, there is always something new to discover.

This website was created as part of my IT Bootcamp and represents my first steps into web development. It is both a learning experience and a personal project, bringing together an old passion and a new ambition.

I hope that exploring this website sparks some of the same curiosity that inspired me to create it.
